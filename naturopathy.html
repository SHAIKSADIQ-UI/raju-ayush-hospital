Naturopathy (Naturopathi) Care

Naturopathy is a holistic, drugless system of medicine based on the principle that the human body possesses an inherent power to heal itself (Vis Medicatrix Naturae). By harmonizing the body with the five great elements of nature (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, and Space), Naturopathy cleanses accumulated morbid matter and re-establishes pristine health.

Core Naturopathic Therapies:

  • Diet & Fasting Therapy: Therapeutic elimination diets, alkaline juicing, and structured fasting to allow internal organs to regenerate.
  • Hydrotherapy & Mud Packs: Application of temperature-controlled water therapies and mineral-rich mud packs to reduce inflammation, improve circulation, and soothe nerves.
  • Metabolic & Lifestyle Correction: Scientific guidance on daily circadian rhythm, sleep hygiene, natural breathing, and stress release.
  • Natural Detoxification: Stimulating the four organs of elimination (skin, lungs, kidneys, and bowels) for thorough systemic cleansing.

Ideal for Treating:

Obesity, Type 2 Diabetes, High Blood Pressure, Chronic Constipation, Gastric bloating, Insomnia, Psoriasis, Gout, and Chronic Lifestyle-induced fatigue.
